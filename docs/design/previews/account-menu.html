Today the account is only in the menu behind ☰: open it, then use the ⋯ at the bottom.

1The whole row opens the menu

Click the avatar, email or role, not a 28px ⋯. The ⌃⌄ icon says "this opens a menu about you". ⋯ usually means "more actions for this item".

As in Slack, Discord, VS Code and most sidebar admin layouts.

2The menu says whose account it is

Email and role at the top, then Sign out. The menu is as wide as the rail and opens above the row, so it covers nothing on the page. Settings or Help would go above Sign out later.

As in GitHub, Google and Linear account menus.

3On a phone, the avatar is top right

The rail is hidden behind ☰ on a phone, so the account was two taps away. The avatar sits at the right of the top bar and opens the same menu.

Where mobile web and native apps put the account.

Why it stays at the bottom on desktop

Apps with a top bar put the account at the top right (GitHub, Google, Jira). Apps with only a sidebar put it at the bottom of the sidebar (Slack, Discord, VS Code). SkillRx chose a sidebar with no top bar on 2026-11-24, the same shell as Human Essentials. Adding a bar just for the avatar would take height from every table and break that match.

Both menus use the design system's menu: 8px corners, 8px by 12px items, gray hover, and Escape to close.
