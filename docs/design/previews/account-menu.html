Before, on a phone, the menu opened behind the drawer, so tapping ⋯ showed nothing.

1The whole row opens the menu

Click the avatar, email or role, not a 28px ⋯. The ⌃⌄ icon says "this opens a menu about you"; ⋯ usually means "more actions for this item". The row turns gray on hover and while the menu is open.

As in Slack, Discord, VS Code and most sidebar admin layouts.

2The menu says whose account it is

Email and role at the top, then Sign out, in the design system's menu: 8px corners, 8px by 12px items, gray hover. It opens above the row, 240px wide, and covers nothing on the page.

As in GitHub, Google and Linear account menus.

3Same on a phone, in the drawer

The account stays at the bottom of the drawer, and the menu opens on top of it. Before, it opened behind the drawer.

As in Linear, Notion and Vercel, which keep the account in the drawer.

Considered and skipped: an avatar at the top right on phones

Google, Apple, GitHub and Atlassian put the avatar at the top right on phones, and it would save a tap. Apps with a sidebar drawer more often keep the account in the drawer, matching their desktop layout, so SkillRx does that.

Why the account is at the bottom on desktop

Apps with a top bar put the account at the top right (GitHub, Google, Jira). Apps with only a sidebar put it at the bottom of the sidebar (Slack, Discord, VS Code). SkillRx chose a sidebar with no top bar on 2026-11-24, the same layout as Human Essentials.

In the code: layouts/_sidebar.html.erb and components/sidebar.css in SkillRx, and <rfg-menu> from design system 0.1.9.
