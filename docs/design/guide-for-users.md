# Using SkillRx: what changed in the new design

SkillRx has a new look. **Nothing about how SkillRx works has changed:** your topics, documents,
tags, providers and beacons are where they were, and you sign in the same way. This guide shows you
where things are now.

## Signing in

Go to the SkillRx home page and select **Sign in** at the top right.

- Enter your **email address** and **password**, then select **Sign in**.
- **Forgot your password?** Select **Forgot your password?** under the password field. Enter your
  email address and we'll send you a link to choose a new one.
- **You stay signed in** on that computer until you sign out. (The old "Remember me" checkbox is
  gone because it didn't do anything: you were always remembered.)
- **No account?** Accounts are created by a SkillRx administrator. Email
  [contact@skillrx.org](mailto:contact@skillrx.org).

If you get "Try again later", you've tried to sign in too many times in a row. Wait three minutes
and try again.

## Finding your way around

The menu runs down the left side of every page.

- **Topics**: the list of educational topics. This is where you start.
- **Beacons** (administrators only): the devices in the field that serve SkillRx content offline.
- **Administration** (administrators only): providers, regions, languages, tags, users, import
  reports and jobs. The group is open to start with; select **Administration** to fold it away.
  **Jobs** opens in a new tab.

The page you're on is highlighted in blue.

**At the bottom of the menu** you'll see who you're signed in as, and whether you're an
administrator or a contributor. To **sign out**, select the **⋯** button next to your name, then
**Sign out**.

**On a phone or a narrow window,** the menu is hidden to leave room for the page. Select the
**☰** button at the top left to open it. Press **Escape**, or tap outside it, to close it.

## Using a keyboard

- The first time you press **Tab** on a page, a **Skip to main content** link appears. Press
  **Enter** to jump past the menu.
- The account menu opens with **Enter** or **Space**. Move through it with the arrow keys, and close
  it with **Escape**.

## What looks different

- One blue (from the SkillRx logo) for buttons, links and the page you're on. Green, yellow and red
  now only mean something: success, a warning, or a problem.
- Form fields have darker outlines, so they're easier to see.
- Messages after you sign in or reset your password appear in the form, right where you're looking,
  not in a corner of the screen.
- The "Built with ❤️" banner no longer covers the bottom of every page. It's at the end of the page
  now.

**Being updated screen by screen.** The topics, beacons and administration pages are next. This
guide will be updated as they change.

## Something wrong?

If something doesn't work the way it did, or you can't find something, email
[contact@skillrx.org](mailto:contact@skillrx.org) with the page you were on and what you expected.
